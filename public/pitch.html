The Solution

Two swarms of autonomous LLM agents that battle continuously against your stack.

Red Swarm — Attackers

Each agent reasons with an LLM about what to attack next, fires real payloads (prompt injection, SQLi, path traversal, XSS), chains exploits, and adapts based on what worked.

Blue Swarm — Defenders

Detects anomalies, deploys validation / WAF rules / rate-limits in real time, and learns which defenses hold — hardening you against an adversary that evolves faster than humans.

Not a scanner running fixed signatures. Agents that reason, learn, and chain — the difference between a chess engine and AlphaGo, applied to offensive security.
